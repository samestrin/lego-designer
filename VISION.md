# Vision

`lego-designer` exists so that anyone can turn a photo into a LEGO mosaic they could really build.
It serves people who enjoy building, and it is a free demo.
It owns exactly one thing: turning a photo into a plate-edge mosaic plan.

## Private and simple

Everything runs in the browser, and no image leaves the machine.
There are no accounts, no server and no payments.

## Honest build plans

The preview shows what the wall will look like.
The build guide and parts count match the preview.

## Scope

It is not a shop, a kit seller or a general image editor.
It is an unofficial project, not affiliated with the LEGO Group.

A change aligns when it makes a plan more buildable or the demo more fun.
A change should be resisted when it adds a server, an account or a cost.
